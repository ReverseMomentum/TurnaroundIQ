**Subject:** Sample and production quote: 3 styles, heavyweight fleece and jersey, screen printed (FTD Drop 01)

Hi [name / team],

I'm [your name], founder of FTD (Forsaken To Decay), a UK streetwear label preparing its first drop. I came across
[factory name] while looking for a manufacturer for heavyweight cut-and-sew with screen printing, and I'd like a
quote for samples and a first production run.

**The styles** (full tech packs attached):

1. **Crossbone Zip Hoodie (FTD-D01-HD01):** cropped boxy zip hoodie, balloon sleeves, 450–500 gsm cotton fleece. One-colour white screen prints on the chest, back and both sleeves.
2. **Crossbone Baggy Pants (FTD-D01-PT01):** barrel-leg joggers in the same fleece. Three one-colour white crosses wrapping the outer side seams.
3. **Never Enough Tee (FTD-D01-TS01):** cropped boxy tee, 260–280 gsm cotton jersey. One-colour white print on the chest and back.

All three are black. I'll confirm the Pantone TCX code and approve a lab dip before bulk. Several prints cross
seams or the zip, so the tech packs ask for those panels to be **printed before sewing**.

**What I'm looking for**
- **Sample:** 1 of each style in size M, plus a print strike-off on the sample fabric.
- **First production run:** around [50] pieces per style, sizes XS–XXL. Please quote at [50] and [100] if your price changes.

**Could you please confirm, in this order:**

1. Sample cost per style, lead time, and how many fit rounds are included. Is the sample cost credited against a bulk order?
2. Minimum order per style and colour, and how it can be split across sizes.
3. Unit price per style at the quantities above, and what that includes.
4. Do you screen print in house? Can you print cut panels before sewing?
5. Screen setup cost per screen and print cost per location. Drop 01 uses six one-colour screens, some shared between styles. The pants crosses wrap the outer side seams, so please quote the print cost per half and say how you match the halves at the seam.
6. Which ink you'd recommend on this fabric (water-based discharge or plastisol), and the cost of a strike-off.
7. Fabric: your 450–500 gsm 100% cotton fleece and 260–280 gsm jersey options, with shrinkage figures. Swatches would be very welcome.
8. Zips: your options for the sample, and the cost and minimum for a custom FTD zip pull in production.
9. Labels: can you source the woven main label, printed care labels and size tabs from our artwork? Artwork is attached.
10. Pattern grading XS–XXL, and whether we can have a copy of the pattern.
11. Production lead time after sample approval.
12. Shipping to the UK (courier and cost). Please include proof of origin on the commercial invoice.
13. Payment terms for samples and for bulk.

Measurements and some materials in the tech packs are marked PROPOSED. If anything looks off for this kind of
garment, I'm very happy to take your advice.

It would also help to see photos of similar heavyweight or screen-printed pieces you've made, and to have a quick
video call before we go ahead.

Thanks very much, and I look forward to hearing from you.

# Drop 01 factory shortlist

October 2026. Everything below is what each factory says about itself on its own site or in roundup
articles, mostly written by other manufacturers. None of it has been checked yet. Confirm minimums,
fabric weights and printing in writing before paying for anything.

Requirements: heavyweight fleece cut-and-sew (450–500 gsm), a custom cropped zip hoodie and barrel-leg
joggers, screen printing on cut panels before sewing, and small runs.

## Before paying anyone

- Ask for photos or video of heavyweight fleece and screen-printed garments they have actually made.
  Better still, a video call walking round the factory.
- Ask for brand references, or check their reviews.
- Get the quote in writing, broken down: sample cost, how many fit rounds, screen setup, print cost,
  unit price per size, minimum, shipping.
- Ask whether the sample fee comes off the bulk order.
- Pay for samples with a protected method (card or PayPal for small amounts), and never pay the full
  bulk amount upfront.
- Imports: goods from Portugal can arrive duty-free if they meet the UK–EU rules of origin. From
  Pakistan or China expect import duty, plus 20% import VAT on everything. Check current rates on GOV.UK
  before comparing prices.

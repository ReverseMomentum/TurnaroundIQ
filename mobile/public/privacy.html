Who is responsible for your data

What we collect

  • Email address — to create your account and send sign-in codes.
  • Account ID — a random identifier (e.g. u_…) linking your data and subscription.
  • Sign-in codes and session tokens — stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • IP address — briefly, to stop abuse of the sign-in form.
  • Your settings and tracked (“paper”) bets — what you enter in the app.
  • Subscription status — whether you have Pro and when it renews, received from RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.

We don’t use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don’t sell your data.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you signed up for and manage your subscription — performance of a contract.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse — legitimate interests.
  • To meet tax and accounting duties for payments — legal obligation.

Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the service, under contracts that protect your data:

  • Contabo — hosts our server (EU data centre).
  • Brevo — sends sign-in code emails.
  • RevenueCat and Stripe — handle subscriptions and payments.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, they use approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in codes: deleted after 24 hours. Sessions: expire after 90 days.
  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records: kept by Stripe/RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting.

Storage on your device

The app stores one item in your browser (your sign-in session) so you stay signed in. It’s strictly necessary for the service, so no cookie banner is required. We don’t use tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account and its data yourself in Settings → Delete account (cancel any subscription first). For anything else, email support@turnaroundiq.co.uk. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Age

TurnaroundIQ is for adults aged 18+. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app or by email.
